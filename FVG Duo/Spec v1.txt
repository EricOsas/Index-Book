====================================================================
2. BARS
====================================================================
All wall-clock logic uses America/New_York (zoneinfo).
Build 5m, 10m and 15m bars from the 1m bars: bar_start = open_ts floored to a multiple of N minutes on ET wall-clock minutes. open = open of the earliest 1m bar in the group, high = max high, low = min low, close = close of the latest 1m bar. bar_end = bar_start + N minutes. Do NOT fill in missing bars. A bar exists only if it contains at least one 1m bar.
Use ALL hours (Globex plus RTH) for all bars. Do not drop pre-market bars. The 10m bars before 09:30 are required for Opening Drive. Trend FVG needs 15m bars ending at 09:30.
"Consecutive" means: b2.start == b1.start + N, and b3.start == b2.start + N. Any triple that is not consecutive (for example across the 17:00-18:00 break, a weekend or a missing bar) is invalid. Log it as reason "non_consecutive".
Trading date = the ET calendar date of the entry bar.

====================================================================
3. FAIR VALUE GAP (the exact definition for every FVG in this document)
====================================================================
Three consecutive bars c1, c2, c3 (c3 is the newest, the one that just closed).
 Bullish FVG: c3.low > c1.high (strict if fvg_strict=True, else >=).
 Bearish FVG: c3.high < c1.low (strict if fvg_strict=True, else <=).
c2 is the middle candle. c2's own prices play no part in the gap test.
Do not use any "rising highs and lows" test. That is a different, wrong thing.

====================================================================
4. INDICATORS (on the 15m all-hours bar series, in time order, no gap filling)
====================================================================
EMA(N): pandas ewm(span=N, adjust=False) on close, seeded with the first close.
True range TR_i = max(high-low, abs(high - prev_close), abs(low - prev_close)), where prev_close is the close of the previous EXISTING 15m bar. For the first bar, TR = high - low.
ATR(14), by atr_method:
 "rma": at index 13 = mean of TR[0..13]; after that, atr_i = (atr_{i-1} * 13 + TR_i) / 14. NaN before index 13.
 "sma": mean of TR[i-13..i]. NaN before index 13.
Warm-up: a Trend signal is invalid if the c3 bar's index in the 15m series is below 1000. Log it as "warmup".

====================================================================
5. OPENING DRIVE FVG (OD)
====================================================================
Uses 10m bars. Process each ET date as follows:
 Check 1: c3 = the 10m bar with start 09:30 (it closes at 09:40). c1 and c2 are the two 10m bars immediately before it (starts 09:10 and 09:20). All three must exist and be consecutive.
   - Bullish FVG -> LONG, entry at 09:40.
   - Bearish FVG -> SHORT, entry at 09:40.
   - No FVG -> go to Check 2.
 Check 2 (only if Check 1 found no FVG): c3 = the 10m bar with start 09:40 (closes 09:50). c1 and c2 start 09:20 and 09:30. Same rules. Entry at 09:50.
 If Check 1 produced a signal, never run Check 2.
 Entry price = the OPEN of the 5m bar whose start equals the entry time (09:40 or 09:50). If that 5m bar does not exist, skip and log "no_entry_bar".
 Stop = entry_price * (1 - od_stop_pct) for LONG, or entry_price * (1 + od_stop_pct) for SHORT. Round to the nearest 0.25 (halves round up). No target. Time exit (section 8).

====================================================================
6. TREND FVG (long only)
====================================================================
Uses 15m bars. For every index k in the 15m series, with c1 = bar k-2, c2 = bar k-1, c3 = bar k, a signal exists only if ALL of these hold:
 a. The three bars are consecutive (section 2).
 b. Bullish FVG per section 3.
 c. Displacement: (c2.high - c2.low) >= trend_atr_mult * ATR, where ATR = atr[k-1] if atr_ref="c2" (the ATR window includes c2), or atr[k-2] if atr_ref="c1" (the window ends at c1). ATR must not be NaN.
 d. Trend: ema_fast[k] > ema_slow[k] (strict), both read at c3's index.
 e. entry_time = c3.bar_end. Its ET time-of-day must be between 09:30 and 15:30 inclusive (trend_last_entry="15:30", inclusive).
 f. Entry price = OPEN of the 5m bar whose start == entry_time. If that bar does not exist, skip and log "no_entry_bar".
 g. stop = c1.low - 0.25. If entry_price <= stop, reject and log "entry_at_or_below_stop".
 h. R = entry_price - stop. target = entry_price + trend_rr * R.
No Trend trade after 15:30 entry time. Time exit per section 8.

====================================================================
7. ONE TRADE'S LIFE: FILL SIMULATION ON 1-MINUTE BARS
====================================================================
A position is entered at entry_time at entry_price, which is the open of the 1m bar whose open_ts == entry_time. Begin checking exits on THAT SAME 1m bar and continue on each later 1m bar of the same ET date with open_ts < 16:00. If a 1m bar does not exist, skip it.
For a LONG, on each 1m bar, check in this order (the first that applies ends the trade):
 1. bar.open <= stop      -> exit at bar.open,  reason "STOP"   (gap through the stop)
 2. bar.low  <= stop      -> exit at stop,      reason "STOP"   (the stop wins ties with the target inside the same bar)
 3. target exists and bar.open >= target -> exit at bar.open, reason "TARGET"
 4. target exists and bar.high >= target -> exit at target,   reason "TARGET"
For a SHORT, mirror it: 1. bar.open >= stop -> exit at open. 2. bar.high >= stop -> exit at stop. (OD shorts have no target.)
Time exit: if no exit occurred, exit at the CLOSE of the last 1m bar of that ET date with open_ts < 16:00. Reason "TIME". On a normal day this is the 15:59 bar. If that last bar's open_ts is earlier than 15:55, still use it but mark the day "short_day" in the log.
exit_ts for STOP/TARGET = open_ts of the 1m bar where it happened. For TIME = last bar open_ts + 1 minute. exit_bar_close = exit bar open_ts + 1 minute (use this for the "flat by" check in section 8).

====================================================================
8. DUO ARBITRATION (the rules for combining OD and Trend; one position at a time)
====================================================================
Engine switches: enable_od, enable_trend. (OD-only, Trend-only and Duo must all be runnable.) In Trend-only mode, Trend may take several trades per day, one at a time.
Per trading date:
 Step 1. Compute the OD signal (maybe none) and the full list of Trend signals (sorted by entry_time). Signal generation does not depend on position state.
 Step 2. Case "no OD signal today": walk the Trend signals in time order. Take a signal only if there is no open position, meaning entry_time >= exit_bar_close of the previous trade. Simulate it (section 7). Repeat until the signal list is exhausted.
 Step 3. Case "OD signal today" at time T_od (09:40 or 09:50):
  3a. Before T_od: walk the Trend signals with entry_time < T_od in order, taking them under the same one-at-a-time rule. Simulate each, but only evaluate its 1m bars with open_ts < T_od. If it exits in that range, it is a normal closed trade. If it has not exited by then, it is OPEN at T_od.
  3b. If no Trend position is open at T_od: the OD trade enters normally at T_od (source "OD").
  3c. If a Trend LONG is open at T_od and OD is LONG (MERGE): do not open a second position. Replace the Trend position's exit rules: remove the target, set the stop to merge_anchor_price * (1 - od_stop_pct) rounded to the nearest 0.25, and keep the time exit. merge_anchor "trend_entry" (default) means the anchor is the Trend position's own entry price. "od_signal" means the anchor is the open of the 5m bar at T_od. If bar.open of the 1m bar at T_od is already <= the new stop, exit at that open with reason "STOP". Resume the section 7 simulation starting at the 1m bar with open_ts == T_od. Record ONE trade, source "MERGED", entry_ts and entry_price from the Trend entry.
  3d. If a Trend LONG is open at T_od and OD is SHORT (FLIP): close the Trend long at the open of the 1m bar at T_od (reason "FLIP"; record it as its own trade, source "TREND"). Then open the OD SHORT at the same price at T_od (source "OD"), with the OD stop.
  3e. After 3b/3c/3d, while the OD or MERGED position is open, take NO Trend signals.
  3f. After the OD, MERGED or FLIP-OD position closes:
    - If trend_after_od_stopout = False (BASELINE; this is the video's rule): no more trades that day.
    - If trend_after_od_stopout = True: if and only if that final OD/MERGED position's exit reason is "STOP", resume walking the remaining Trend signals with entry_time >= exit_bar_close of that position (one at a time, as in Step 2). Exits by TIME mean the day is over. Mark those trades with after_od_stopout = 1.
 Trend signals are never carried over to the next day.

====================================================================
9. COSTS
====================================================================
Fees: 2.14 per side. Each trade pays 2 sides (4.28). A FLIP is two separate trades, so 4 sides in total. A MERGED trade is ONE trade, 2 sides.
Slippage: slip_ticks (default 0) ticks per fill, applied adversely to every fill (entries, stops, targets, time exits, FLIP exits). For a LONG, entry_fill = entry_price + slip, exit_fill = exit_price - slip. For a SHORT, the reverse. Stop and target levels are computed from the UNSLIPPED entry_price.
points = (exit_fill - entry_fill) for a LONG, (entry_fill - exit_fill) for a SHORT. gross_usd = points * 20. net_usd = gross_usd - fees.
